# Human anatomy model attribution

`z-anatomy-1.2.0-full-body.glb`, `z-anatomy-1.4.0-muscular.glb`, `z-anatomy-cerebrum.glb`, and `z-anatomy-cerebellum.glb` are browser-oriented adaptations of the [Z-Anatomy atlas](https://github.com/Z-Anatomy/Models), downloaded from [Vanatome's public model release](https://github.com/vixotic/Vanatome/tree/main/public/models). The assets are licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

Credit: Z-Anatomy contributors, especially Gauthier Kervyn (design, 3D, anatomy) and Marcin Zielinski (Python script), with additional contributors and upstream sources documented by Z-Anatomy. The atlas includes work derived from [BodyParts3D / DBCLS](https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/download.html). Vanatome adapted the asset for web export, structure selection identifiers, materials, and geometry optimization; see its [asset licensing notice](https://github.com/vixotic/Vanatome/blob/main/ASSET-LICENSE.md).

This project redistributes the Vanatome GLBs without changing their geometry or materials. `src/features/biology-lab/data/anatomyParts.json` is a derived, sorted list of internal anatomy and muscle names and systems, with the cerebrum and cerebellum grouped for the viewer. The viewer uses selected body-surface meshes as a tinted presentation shell, excludes the urogenital surface and pubic hair, and adds a runtime ordered layout, selection highlighting, and procedural muscle-fibre shading and tinting of materials. These adapted asset data remain under CC BY-SA 4.0.
